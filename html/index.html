WHY THIS NUMBER?

The button chooses when the wash ends.

  1. 01

    The machine accepts only whole-hour settings from 3 to 19 hours.

  2. 02

    We subtract the programme time to check that the wash starts after cheap energy begins.

  3. 03

    We centre the cycle in the window where possible, leaving room for real-world timing drift.

Official programme-time referenceSamsung manual · page 37

Quick wash can be selected at 15, 20, 30, 40, 50 or 60 minutes. Actual times can vary with water pressure and temperature, load, fabric and selected options. Intensive wash extends every cycle; use the measured-time field when you use it. Official Samsung support page.
